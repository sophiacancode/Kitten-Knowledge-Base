
Food enrichment gives cats something to search for or figure out at mealtimes. A simple puzzle or a few hidden portions can turn part of a normal meal into an activity.

## Foraging Activities

- Put a little dry food in the compartments of an open cardboard egg carton.
- Hide small portions in easy-to-find places.
- Offer food in a puzzle feeder designed for cats.

### Introducing a Food Puzzle

Start with an open egg carton and leave the food visible. Show your cat where it is, then let them investigate and try to get it out.

Once your cat can retrieve the food easily, gradually make the puzzle more challenging. For example, you could partially close the carton lid rather than leaving it fully open.

> Start with an easy puzzle and increase the difficulty gradually.

## Managing Portions

Use food from your cat's daily allowance rather than adding extra portions for enrichment. Set aside some of their usual food for puzzles or hiding activities.
