# Environmental and Vertical Space

Environmental enrichment gives cats places to hide, perch, scratch, rest, and explore. Vertical space makes use of height rather than just floor space. It also gives cats another way to move around and spend time away from other pets.

## Useful Features

- Cat trees and stable elevated resting places
- Cardboard boxes or other accessible hiding spaces
- Scratching posts and pads
- Quiet resting areas

### Planning an Accessible Layout

Choose structures that are stable and easy for your cat to reach. Not every resting place needs to be high up: provide lower options for cats that have difficulty jumping.

Consider how the spaces connect, too. In homes with multiple cats, elevated routes can help cats move around without having to pass close to one another.
